Have Questions?

How far in advance should I book?

We recommend booking at least 2-3 months in advance to ensure availability, especially for weddings and major events. However, we do accept last-minute bookings when possible.

What's included in your packages?

Our packages include professional photography/videography, edited photos and videos, and digital copies. Specific inclusions vary by package - please check our event pages for details.

How long does editing take?

Typical turnaround time is 2-4 weeks for photos and 4-6 weeks for videos. Rush editing services are available for an additional fee.

Do you offer refunds?

Refund policies vary based on how far in advance you cancel. Please refer to our terms and conditions or contact us directly for specific information.

Can we customize our package?

Yes! We're happy to work with you to create a custom package that meets your specific needs and budget. Contact us to discuss your requirements.
